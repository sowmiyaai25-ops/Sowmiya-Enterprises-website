About Sowmiya Enterprises

Family-Managed Distribution Business Based in Tirupati

Established in 2018 in the holy pilgrimage city of Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, Sowmiya Enterprises is a trusted family-managed distribution enterprise founded and owned by Mr. V. Sundareshan, supported in operations by Sangeetha.

We specialize in supplying genuine chocolates, coffee powders, beverage premixes, milk powder, and commercial coffee vending machine solutions to business and institutional customers across Andhra Pradesh.

Family-Managed Integrity

Direct oversight by V. Sundareshan & Sangeetha ensures prompt delivery and genuine product guarantees.
